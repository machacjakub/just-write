# JustWrite

A blank page. A word count. Colorful bars that quietly judge your progress.

That's it. That's the app.

**Try it:** https://machacjakub.github.io/just-write/

## What it does

- **You type.** Words appear. Wonderful.
- **Bars fill up** at the top as you write: green to 800 words, then blue to 1200, then yellow to 1600. Change the goals and colors in settings (⚙), or add more bars. Five is plenty. Nobody needs six.
- **Open and save real files** on your computer. Click the icons, or use `⌘/Ctrl + O` and `⌘/Ctrl + S`. A little dot on the save icon tells you if your file is up to date:
  - 🟠 you changed something, save it
  - 🟢 all good
- **Autosaves a draft** in your browser, so closing the tab by accident is not a tragedy.
- **Dark mode** comes for free. Your eyes say thanks.

## Heads up

Saving straight to a file needs a browser that allows it: Chrome, Edge, Arc, or Brave. Safari and Firefox can open files but will download a copy instead of saving over the original.

**Brave** hides this feature by default. Turn it on at `brave://flags/#file-system-access-api`, then relaunch.

## Privacy

Your words stay in your browser and on your disk. No server, no account, no tracking. There is nowhere for them to go.

## Run it yourself

It's one HTML file. Open `index.html`. Done. No install, no build, no `node_modules` black hole.

## Why?

Because most writing apps want to be everything. This one just wants you to write.
